# User Guide

## Browsing games

Go to **Games** in the nav bar. All games are listed in a table.

To search: type in the search box and press Filter.
To filter by genre: pick a genre from the dropdown and press Filter.
To clear filters: click **Clear**.

## Adding a game

1. Click **Add Game** in the nav bar.
2. Fill in Title (required) and Genre (required).
3. Optionally add a Description and upload a Cover Image (JPG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP).
4. Click **Submit**.

## Editing a game

On the Games page, click **Edit** next to a game. Update the fields, then click **Update Game**.

Note: editing does not replace the cover image. Image replacement is not yet supported.

## Deleting a game

On the Games page, click **Delete** next to a game. Confirm in the browser prompt. The game is removed immediately.

## Viewing members

Click **Members** in the nav bar to see the group list. Click a name for their profile.
